01 · Surviving text + recovered context

Read the text.
Then recover the place.

The Greek remains fixed. The contextual horizon changes. This view separates what Plato explicitly says from material, spatial and institutional knowledge recoverable outside the surviving dialogue.

What is explicit?

What is implicit?

The world the text does not enumerate.

These records are not inserted into Plato’s wording. They are independently recoverable aspects of the setting that may have been available to some contemporary receivers.

Methodological rule
Absence from the surviving dialogue does not imply absence from the dramatic environment. Every implicit item remains separately labelled by evidence type and uncertainty.
